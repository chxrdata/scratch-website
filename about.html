Del Cro Halter is an information and graphic designer based in Brooklyn, New York. Their practice sits at the intersection of natural systems and human curiosity, bridging the gap between the spaces we inhabit and the information interwoven between them. They work across museums, journalism, nonprofits, and the web to inspire education and positive change. They currently work at Ralph Appelbaum Associates.

Del is also a gardener and a writer. This website was lovingly hand-coded, inspired in part by The Algorithmic Beauty of Plants (where the fractal drawing algorithm was sourced). Fractal shapes were modeled on plants native to Florida and New York.
